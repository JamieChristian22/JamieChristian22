# Work with me — funded maintenance, digital products, and remote opportunities

I'm open to discussing **legitimate, clearly scoped paid collaborations** related to the public work in my [GitHub portfolio](README.md). These are possible ways to work together; this page is not a claim of existing paid clients, corporate contracts, active bounties, or guaranteed service capacity.

## 1. Company-funded maintenance & scoped bounties

Organizations, project maintainers, and community partners can propose funded work such as:

- Improving documentation, reproducibility, accessibility, test coverage, and example setup instructions.
- Fixing validated bugs or strengthening data-quality checks in an agreed project.
- Developing small educational sample workflows or reusable technical examples.
- Providing documented maintenance or support for a project **only after an agreed scope and support terms**.

**Before work starts:** identify the repository and issue, confirm rights and technical authorization, specify deliverables and acceptance criteria, set the fee or funded bounty, agree on payment/milestones and any maintenance period, and confirm an authorized point of contact. Do not treat a GitHub issue label as a funded bounty unless funding and payment terms are verifiable.

Work involving private systems, regulated data, credentials, or production infrastructure needs separate authorization and security review. Unsolicited vulnerability reports and security-sensitive disclosures should follow each repository's security policy, not a public paid-work issue.
