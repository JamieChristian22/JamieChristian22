### How pricing and delivery work

- **Request a project quote:** Describe the deliverable, budget range, deadline, authorized data sources, and any licensing requirements. Pricing depends on scope and is confirmed **before** work begins; no unverified price or turnaround time is promised here.
- **Written agreement first:** Agree on deliverables, revision limits, milestone payments, confidentiality, licensing, and acceptance criteria. A consultation or GitHub issue does not itself commission paid work.
- **Remote-only delivery:** Work is discussed and completed remotely. No unapproved production access, restricted customer records, or compliance assurances.
- **Current service catalog:** See the listings actually available on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/jamiechristian). Only the terms shown in a confirmed listing or agreed quote govern purchases.
